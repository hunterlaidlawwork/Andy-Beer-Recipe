About Andy

This guide is built to keep Andy’s way of brewing alive. Simple process. No shortcuts. Good beer every time.

⚠ Golden Rule

Sanitize everything that touches your beer. Every time.

01 — Brew Day

  • Sanitize everything
  • Add water, malt, and dextrose
  • Cool below 30°C
  • Add yeast
  • Seal and leave it alone

02 — Transfer

  • Siphon gently into carboy
  • Keep sediment out
  • Add airlock

Leave 10–14 days. Don’t rush it.

03 — Bottling

  • Add priming sugar first
  • Siphon beer in (no splashing)
  • Fill bottles fully
  • Cap immediately

Airlock must be still 48+ hours before bottling.

Conditioning

  • Store at 18–22°C
  • Wait at least 2 weeks
  • Test one bottle before chilling all

Timeline

  • Day 0 — Brew
  • Day 9–14 — Transfer
  • Day 19–24 — Bottle
  • +2 Weeks — Ready
